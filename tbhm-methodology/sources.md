## Provenance & currency policy

- The *workflow* is the durable content — competition-shaped discovery →
  mapping → tactical testing stays valid; named tools/sources are
  era-marked (`> **…note:**` callouts per chapter) and route to
  `recon-pipeline`'s version-verified equivalents.
- Volumetric steps (port scans, directory brute-force, fuzzing) are
  permission- and rate-gated per `recon-pipeline` class labels — TBHM's
  speed mindset does not override scope or policy.
- Proof ceilings per class live in the tactical chapters and in
  `payloads-all-the-things` callouts.
